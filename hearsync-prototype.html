System architecture

The signal path from raw sound to a directional vibration, block by block.

1MEMS Microphone Array
4–5 small digital microphones positioned around the watch body capture ambient sound continuously.
↓
2Audio Processingsignal processing
Raw microphone signals are filtered and prepared — noise reduction and level normalization — before analysis.
↓
3AI Sound ClassificationAI / ML
A trained model identifies what the sound is — e.g. emergency siren, vehicle horn, alarm, speech — and outputs a confidence score.
↓
4TDOA Direction Detectionsignal processing
Comparing the tiny arrival-time differences across microphones estimates which direction the sound came from. This is a geometry calculation, not a learned model.
↓
5Priority Engine
Combines sound type and urgency rules to decide which detected sound — if any — deserves the user's attention right now.
↓
6Microcontroller
Receives the classification, direction, and priority decision, then drives the correct haptic motor.
↓
7Haptic Motors
Directional vibration motors on the left, right, front, and rear of the band alert the user to where the sound is coming from.

Microphone array

Multiple microphones allow the system to compare the arrival time of the same sound and estimate its approximate direction.

5 mics active
M1 — top bezel (12 o'clock)
M2 — upper right (2 o'clock)
M3 — lower right (4 o'clock)
M4 — bottom bezel (6 o'clock)
M5 — upper left (10 o'clock)

A sound arrives at the nearest microphone a fraction of a millisecond before the others. That tiny time gap, compared across all five mics, is what the direction-detection stage uses to work out where the sound came from.

Priority engine

Turns a sound type and direction into a decision about whether — and how strongly — to alert the user.

Sound typeDirectionPriorityAlert
Emergency SirenLeftHIGHStrong left vibration
Vehicle HornRightMEDIUMRight vibration
ConversationFrontLOWNo alert
The priority engine prevents the user from receiving unnecessary alerts for every environmental sound. When several sounds are detected at once, it selects the single most important one — typically the highest-urgency, safety-relevant sound — so the wearer gets one clear signal instead of constant, low-value buzzing.
